Hermes Personal Assistant

A privately operated personal assistant for managing its owner's Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive. This is a personal integration operated by the GitHub account MatthewCHarrison, not a public sign-up service or an official Google product.

What it does

  • Find and summarise email, prepare replies, and organise messages.
  • Review schedules and help manage calendar events.
  • Find and work with files in Google Drive.

Access is granted through Google OAuth. The assistant is instructed to obtain the owner's approval before sending messages or changing account data. This is an operating rule, not a claim that Google permissions are read-only.

The assistant may use selected AI and other service providers to process information needed for a requested task.

Read the privacy policy for details of data access, processing, storage and revocation.
