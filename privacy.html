Privacy policy

This policy describes the personal Google-account integration named Hermes Personal Assistant, operated by the owner of the GitHub account MatthewCHarrison. It is intended for the operator's personal use, not public registration.

Google data accessed and purpose

With the account owner's authorization, the integration can access Gmail messages, message metadata and labels; calendar information and events; and Drive files and metadata. It uses this access to carry out requested email, scheduling and document-management tasks.

The granted permissions permit reading and modifying Gmail, including sending messages, managing calendars, and reading and managing Drive files. The assistant is instructed to ask for approval before sending messages or changing account data.

Processing and sharing

Google account data is processed to provide the requested assistant functions. Relevant information may be shared with selected AI and other service providers as needed to perform those tasks, subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices. This integration does not sell Google account data or use it for advertising.

Storage and retention

Authorization credentials and task-related information may be retained to maintain access, support ongoing work and preserve interaction history. Copies may also exist in logs and backups. Access is restricted through the available account and system controls. Retention depends on configured settings and deletion practices; no fixed deletion period is guaranteed.

Control, revocation and deletion

Access can be revoked through Google Account connections. Revoking access prevents further authorized access but does not automatically erase information already retained. Stored information can be removed through the relevant application, storage and provider controls, with backups subject to their retention arrangements. Disconnecting the integration does not delete the original data held by Google.

This public website

These information pages do not contain Google account content, credentials or a sign-in form. They include no custom analytics or tracking scripts. If hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes website access information, including visitor IP addresses, under its privacy statement.

Contact and changes

Contact the operator through their GitHub profile. Do not disclose private account information in public messages. This policy should be revised when the integration's processing practices change.
